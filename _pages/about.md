<p style="text-align: justify; font-size: 15px;">This is <strong>Md. Mahbubul Kabir Nishat</strong>. I am a <strong>Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering</strong> and a <strong>Graduate Research Assistant</strong> in the <a href="https://www.imse.iastate.edu/" target="_blank"><strong>Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Systems Engineering</strong></a> at <a href="https://www.iastate.edu/" target="_blank"><strong>Iowa State University</strong></a>, where I work with my advisor, <a href="https://www.engineering.iastate.edu/people/profile/estherjo" target="_blank"><strong>Dr. Esther Jose</strong></a>.
</p>
<p style="text-align: justify; font-size: 15px;">My research focuses on <strong><em>data-driven optimization</em></strong> and <strong><em>geostatistical modeling under uncertainty</em></strong>, especially in <strong><em>emergencies</em></strong>. I apply <strong><em>machine learning</em></strong> to <strong><em>disaster risk mitigation</em></strong>, <strong><em>healthcare optimization</em></strong>, and <strong><em>supply chain resilience</em></strong>. Currently, my work combines <strong><em>operations research</em></strong> and <strong><em>machine learning</em></strong> for <strong><em>UAV network design</em></strong>.
</p>
<p style="text-align: justify; font-size: 15px;">Before starting my Ph.D., I worked at <strong>PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) Bangladesh</strong> as an SAP consultant, leading the implementation of <strong>Supply Chain and Manufacturing modules</strong> for clients in the pharmaceutical, textile, electronics, and chemical manufacturing sectors. I completed my <strong>M.Sc. in Industrial & Production Engineering (IPE) at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)</strong>, where I served as a <strong>Teaching Assistant</strong>, and my <strong>B.Sc. in IPE at Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET)</strong>, where I received the <strong>Dean's Award and University Merit Scholarship</strong>. I have also served as a <strong>Guest Lecturer at the National Institute of Textile Engineering and Research (NITER)</strong>.
</p>
